About this map

This map shows how flood scenarios could affect mobility in Turku. Choose a climate scenario (RCP 4.5 = moderate, RCP 8.5 = high emissions) to see flood depth, roads at risk and Donkey Republic stations located within a 100 m flood buffer (shown in grey).

Data: flood risk maps (Lounaistieto / IKI project), Donkey Republic stations, road risk layers.
