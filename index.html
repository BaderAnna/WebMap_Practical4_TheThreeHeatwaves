Background information about this map

The map visualises two different future flood scenarios by the year 2100 and how those are impacting Föli mobility, more explicitly bike, bus and ferry stations. Two different RCP (Representative Concentration Pathway) scenarios are applied. RCP4.5 is a moderate scenario where emissions rise until mid-century and decline afterwards, resulting in moderate sea-level rise and precipitation increases. RCP8.5 is a high-emissions, worst-case scenario with steadily rising emissions, resulting in extreme sea-level rise and severe flooding events.

These RCP scenarios are visible in the map through classifications of flood water depth and road vulnerability risk due to flooding. The flood depth layer shows different flood depth classes:

  • Class 1: 10.1 - 20 cm (low risk)
  • Class 2: 20.1 - 30 cm (low risk)
  • Class 3: 30.1 - 40 cm (moderate risk)
  • Class 4: 40.1 - 50 cm (high risk)
  • Class 5: > 50.1 cm (high risk)

The connected road risk vulnerability layer includes only classes 3-5, the higher risk classes. Those classes, in combination with a 100m buffer zone, are applied to the different station layers to visualise stations that are unavailable due to flooding in the respective scenario.

  • Föli Bike Stations: Shows Donkey Republic city bike stations. The buffer zone of 100m protects bike users from being endangered by broader flood zones.
  • Föli Bus Stations: Shows available bus stops and a 100m buffer zone interlinked with the possible flood scenarios causing accessibility issues for buses or passengers.
  • Föli Water Bus Stations: Shows Föli ferry stations and includes a 100m flood buffer zone for potential users.
